Research areas

What we study.

Agent coordination

Negotiation, task allocation and conflict resolution in decentralized agent contours under real-time constraints.

Industrial standardization

Formal contracts, auditability and interoperability requirements for multi-agent systems in regulated industries.

Agentic re-architecture

Methods for rebuilding legacy industrial software — telematics, dispatch, compliance — as agent-based decision systems.

Human-agent contours

Monitoring and decision-support loops where human operators and autonomous agents share responsibility.

Open standard

MAIF — Multi-Agent Industrial Framework.

An open framework for building auditable industrial agent systems: agent contracts, communication protocol, orchestration semantics. Free to use, fork and implement.

The standard behind our pilots.

Every CIRIYC PRO contour is built on MAIF. The framework is registered, versioned and published in full — specification, reference architecture and examples.
