Research areas

What we study.

Agent coordination

Negotiation, task allocation and conflict resolution in decentralised agent contours under real-time constraints.

Industrial standardisation

Formal contracts, auditability and interoperability requirements for multi-agent systems in regulated industries.

Agentic re-architecture

Methods for rebuilding legacy industrial software — telematics, dispatch, compliance — as agent-based decision systems.

Human-agent contours

Monitoring and decision-support loops where human operators and autonomous agents share responsibility.

Open standard

MAIF — Multi-Agent Industrial Framework.

An open framework for building auditable industrial agent systems: agent contracts, communication protocol, orchestration semantics. Free to use, fork and implement.

The standard behind our pilots.

Every CIRIYC PRO contour is built on MAIF. The framework is registered, versioned and published in full — specification, reference architecture and examples.

Process industry stands

Four MAIF prototypes for chemical and polymer processes. Each runs in your browser; models and data are simulated or synthetic, and every page says exactly what is real.

Stand 1

Digital twin of a gas-phase PE reactor

Reactor, grade chemistry and a 2D bed section computed live: agents watch the sintering margin of the grade being made, catch hot zones before the bed mean moves, and plan grade transitions with less off-spec.

Open stand
Stand 2

Agentic monitoring of a C2 hydrogenation reactor

Six agents watch a simulated tail-end reactor in real time: anomaly detection, heat balance, 30-minute forecast, optimiser, and an operator who approves every action.

Open stand
Stand 3

Certified acceleration of reactor CFD

A surrogate answers in milliseconds; a verifier agent bounds the error of every answer and escalates to the solver when the bound is too wide. No unverified number leaves the system.

Open stand
Stand 4

From source documents to a converged PFD

Replay of a real run on an external LLM: agents extract the design basis, resolve conflicts, propose and simulate three flowsheets, and cross-check the model's numbers against the simulator.
