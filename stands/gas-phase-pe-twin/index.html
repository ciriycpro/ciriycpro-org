Hybrid CFD developed with Borealis Innovation predicts process failures such as chunking and sheeting in an olefin polymerization fluidized-bed reactor. Recurrence CFD then propagates polymerization kinetics and reaction heat far beyond the recorded simulation. Borealis is a partner of the Christian Doppler Laboratory for multi-scale modelling of multiphase processes at JKU.

Sources & parameters

Every number has a source, a derivation or a design range.

Model structure is taken from the open literature listed below. verified: the value is in the cited source. derived: computed or fitted from sourced data; the formula and the target are under the parameter. design: set by the specific plant (equipment, DCS tuning, procedures); the value is inside the stated range and is replaced by the customer’s data in the pilot. No parameter is an unsupported assumption.

What this page is. Computed live: the reactor model (two-site kinetics, gas loop with cooler and condensation, bed temperature control), instantaneous and cumulative polymer properties, the stickiness temperature and safe window, the transition planner and all agents. Illustrative: the bed section stands in for the CFD-trained layer of the pilot; see “Proven in industry” for the published cases this approach follows. Numbers: each parameter in the table above is verified against a source, derived from sourced data (formula given), or a plant design value inside a stated range; fitted scale factors reproduce published industrial production rates and grade data. The stickiness correlation was developed for metallocene resins; for Ziegler–Natta resins it is shown as is and is to be calibrated in the pilot. Grade names are generic.
